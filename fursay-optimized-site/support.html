Help with free stories, parent guides, PDF samples, and adult email updates

Fursay Family Learning Editorial Team · Updated: 2026-09-25

Contact the team

Email contact@fursay.com with the page URL, language, and whether your question concerns Koko or Nour. Do not include a child's identifying or health information.

Printing a sample

Tell us the PDF page, paper size, printer setting, device, and browser. Current samples are formatted for US Letter; screenshots should have personal information removed.

Email updates

An adult subscriber can unsubscribe from any message. For another list request, write from the subscribed address and do not include private information about a child.

Using the stories and guides

Stories, parent guides, and printable samples are free to use for family activities. Contact us if a link, download, or page is not working as expected.

Future services

If Fursay adds paid resources, the price, support route, and applicable order terms will be shown before an order is accepted.

Send me

For parents and caregivers aged 18+. Newsletter emails are in Chinese only — there is no English newsletter. Choose which story world you want updates about. Occasional, at most one email a week. We only use your email address to send these emails; unsubscribe any time. Privacy.
