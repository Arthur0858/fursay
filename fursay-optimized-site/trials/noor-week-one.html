Noor · Try one calm family week

A free prototype for an adult and child to use together. Choose one three-minute activity each day. Skip any day or stop when either person wants to. The full pack is not available yet.

Three words

你好 nǐ hǎo · 谢谢 xiè xie · 再见 zài jiàn

Nour and Zayd meet a friend, say hello, thank a helper, and wave goodbye. A smile and one short attempt are enough.

Day 1

Read the story and wave hello.

Day 2

Point to 你好 and say nǐ hǎo together.

Day 3

Say 谢谢, xiè xie, when someone helps.

Day 4

Wave and say 再见, zài jiàn, when a toy leaves.

Day 5

Match greeting, thanks and goodbye to three toy scenes.

Day 6

Make a short hello-and-goodbye story with two toys.

Day 7

Repeat a favorite word and activity. Tell the adult what was easy or hard.
