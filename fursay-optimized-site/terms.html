Fursay terms of use

How to use free family stories, guides, downloads, and links

Fursay Family Learning Editorial Team · Updated: 2026-09-25

Free family resources

Stories, parent guides, and PDF samples are currently available for personal family use. Please keep attribution and do not resell or redistribute the files as a competing product.

Adult email updates

An adult parent or caregiver may choose to submit an email address for free story updates. Each message includes an unsubscribe option. Do not submit a child's contact details.

Affiliate recommendations

Some optional book links are sponsored affiliate links. Fursay may earn a commission without changing the retailer's price; availability, shipping, and purchase terms belong to the retailer.

General information

Our guides share general parent-facing ideas. They do not guarantee language learning, replace school instruction, or provide medical, developmental, legal, or individualized professional advice.

If paid resources are introduced

Before accepting any paid order, Fursay will display the price, support details, refund terms, and applicable purchase conditions. Families can review those terms before deciding whether to order.

Updates

We may update site content and these terms. Material editorial changes receive a visible modification date and correction route.
