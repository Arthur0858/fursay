Nour in Chinese: name and pronunciation

Nour’s Chinese name in the Fursay story is 努爾 (Nǔ'ěr). Zayd is 扎伊德 (Zhāyīdé).

Fursay Family Learning Editorial Team · Published: 2026-11-27

Read the names with pinyin

Nour (نور) is written 努爾, with the pinyin Nǔ'ěr. Zayd (زيد) is written 扎伊德, with the pinyin Zhāyīdé. The marks above the vowels indicate tones; the apostrophe separates syllables in Nǔ'ěr.

Names in the story

These are the Chinese names of Fursay story characters. They are a way to recognize the characters while reading, not a suggestion to change a child’s name.
