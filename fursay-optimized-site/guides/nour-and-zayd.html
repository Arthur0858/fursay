Who are Nour and Zayd? A parent’s guide

Nour and Zayd appear together in Fursay’s Arabic–Chinese greeting story. The Chinese page calls Nour 努爾; the Arabic page uses نور and زيد. Follow the story with your child and use the language your family knows best.

Nour story page

Start with greetings

The greetings episode brings together Chinese words and Pinyin. Watch together, then try a greeting or a wave. The door guide offers a way to use 你好 and 再见 during arrivals and departures.

Greeting episode · Door greeting guide

Explore colors

The colors story introduces Chinese color words. The table guide suggests pointing to familiar, safe objects and choosing a color together. A child can point or simply watch.
