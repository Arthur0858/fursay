Fursay editorial method

How we choose, write, review, and correct parent-facing content

Fursay · Updated: 2026-08-01

Choose a real question

Every guide starts with one situation a parent may encounter during story time. We avoid manufacturing a broad promise and define one practical outcome that can be tried without buying anything.

Use accountable sources

We prefer official public-health, education, language, or original research sources. Each long guide explains why a source is relevant. Sources inform a principle; they do not prove a Fursay activity will produce a result.

Write for the adult

Instructions address a parent or caregiver, keep the home language available, and accept nonverbal participation. We do not ask a child to create an account or submit a name, age, school, photo, or contact detail.

Review claims and limits

Before release we check titles, examples, sources, commercial links, accessibility, translations, and structured data. We remove guaranteed outcomes and add a limitation whenever a reader might mistake family guidance for assessment or treatment.

Corrections and revisions

Each guide has a visible update date and unique revision note. Readers can report a sentence through Contact. Material corrections update the visible note, Article schema, sitemap lastmod, and AdSense stability window.
