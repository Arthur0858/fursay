About Fursay

A parent-facing source of family stories, guides, and free printables

Fursay Family Learning Editorial Team · Updated: 2026-09-25

How the story worlds began

Fursay grew from a family interest in calmer shared reading. Koko offers English story moments for Mandarin-speaking households; Nour and Zayd create small Chinese story moments that Arabic-speaking caregivers can adapt.

Who uses this site

The site, newsletter, guides, and printable resources are intended for adult parents and caregivers. Children may join an adult for a story, but Fursay does not offer child accounts or ask children to submit personal information.

What is available

Families can read stories and parent guides, download free PDF samples, and choose to receive adult email updates. Book recommendations are optional and disclosed where affiliate links appear.

Who is responsible for the content

The Fursay Family Learning Editorial Team plans topics, checks cited sources, reviews English, Traditional Chinese, and Arabic copy, and publishes the site. Readers can use Contact to report a correction. We do not claim individual credentials or child-testing results.

Our limits

We offer general family reading ideas, not language, health, developmental, legal, or educational assessments. Each guide describes its limits and links to sources that support a principle without claiming they endorse Fursay.
